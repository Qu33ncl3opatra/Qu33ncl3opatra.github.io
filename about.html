About Us

The story behind every sweet creation

Our Story

🎂

Wiggy Baking was born on a rainy Sunday afternoon in 2015, when our founder Amara Wiggins pulled out her grandmother's flour-dusted recipe book and decided, for once, to follow every single instruction to the letter. The result was a lopsided but transcendent Victoria sponge that her neighbours still talk about.

What started as weekend hobby quickly became something more. Friends began requesting cakes for birthdays. Then neighbours for anniversaries. Then a local café asked if Amara could supply them with pastries three mornings a week. Before long, the spare bedroom had been converted into a licensed home kitchen, the garage into a walk-in refrigerator, and Wiggy Baking had officially opened its doors.

Today, we operate from our purpose-built bakery in the heart of the community, serving thousands of customers each year with the same passion, precision, and pinch of playfulness that went into that very first sponge cake. Every recipe we use carries a piece of family history — handed down through generations, adapted with care, and made fresh every single day.

We believe that a beautiful cake doesn't just taste good — it marks a moment. It says: this occasion matters, and so do you. That belief is baked into everything we do.

Est. 2015 🎂

Meet the Team

👩‍🍳

The passionate people behind every delicious bite

👩‍🍳

Amara Wiggins

Founder & Head Baker

Amara launched Wiggy Baking from her kitchen in 2015. With a background in patisserie from the London School of Baking and an unstoppable creative spirit, she leads the team with warmth, precision, and an encyclopaedic knowledge of flavour combinations. Her signature is always a hidden edible flower.

🎨

Sophie Aldridge

Cake Designer

Sophie's background in fine art makes her the most visually adventurous member of our team. She transforms cakes into edible sculptures — from hand-painted watercolour tiers to sculpted fondant landscapes. Her work has been featured in three national wedding magazines and she was a finalist on a well-known television baking show.

🥐

James Okafor

Pastry Specialist

James spent five years training in Lyon, France before bringing his classical pastry skills back home. His macarons are legendary — he once made 1,200 in a single day for a corporate event and still had the energy to test a new pistachio ganache recipe. He holds the office record for most croissants eaten in a sitting: seven.

📋

Priya Nair

Event Coordinator

Priya is the calm, capable force who ensures that every order arrives on time, every event runs smoothly, and every client feels looked after. She has coordinated over 300 events — from intimate birthday parties to grand wedding receptions for 400 guests. Her planning spreadsheets are works of art in themselves.
