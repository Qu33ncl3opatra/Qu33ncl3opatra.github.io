Vanilla Celebration Cake

Ingredients

  • 250g unsalted butter, softened
  • 250g caster sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 tbsp milk
  • For frosting: 150g butter, 300g icing sugar, 2 tsp vanilla

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C. Grease two 20cm tins.
  2. Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy (5 min).
  3. Beat in eggs one at a time, then vanilla.
  4. Fold in flour alternating with milk.
  5. Divide between tins, bake 25–30 min.
  6. Cool completely, then frost and sandwich together.
⏱ 75 min ⭐ Easy

Chocolate Fudge Cake

Ingredients

  • 200g dark chocolate (70%)
  • 200g unsalted butter
  • 200g golden caster sugar
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 100g plain flour
  • 50g cocoa powder
  • For ganache: 200ml double cream, 200g dark chocolate

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 160°C. Grease a 23cm tin.
  2. Melt chocolate and butter together, cool slightly.
  3. Beat in sugar and egg yolks.
  4. Fold in flour and cocoa.
  5. Whisk egg whites to stiff peaks, fold in gently.
  6. Bake 35–40 min. Cool, then pour over warm ganache.
⏱ 90 min ⭐ Medium

Red Velvet Cupcakes

Ingredients

  • 180g plain flour
  • 150g caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp red food colouring
  • 120ml buttermilk
  • 120ml vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs · 1 tsp vanilla · 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • Cream cheese frosting: 250g cream cheese, 100g icing sugar, 1 tsp vanilla

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C. Line 12-hole tin.
  2. Mix dry ingredients. Whisk wet ingredients separately.
  3. Combine gently, don't overmix.
  4. Fill cases ⅔ full. Bake 20–22 min.
  5. Cool completely. Pipe cream cheese frosting on top.
⏱ 50 min ⭐ Easy

Lemon Drizzle Cake

Ingredients

  • 225g unsalted butter, softened
  • 225g caster sugar · 4 eggs
  • 225g self-raising flour
  • Zest of 2 lemons
  • Drizzle: Juice of 1½ lemons + 85g caster sugar

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C. Grease a 900g loaf tin.
  2. Beat butter and sugar until pale and fluffy.
  3. Beat in eggs gradually, fold in flour and zest.
  4. Bake 45–50 min until golden.
  5. Mix lemon juice and sugar. Pour over hot cake.
  6. Leave to cool in tin before slicing.
⏱ 60 min ⭐ Easy

Strawberry Cheesecake

Ingredients

  • Base: 200g digestive biscuits, 100g melted butter
  • Filling: 600g cream cheese, 150g icing sugar, 300ml double cream, 1 tsp vanilla
  • Topping: 300g strawberries, 2 tbsp strawberry jam, 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Crush biscuits, mix with butter, press into base of 23cm tin. Chill 30 min.
  2. Beat cream cheese and icing sugar until smooth.
  3. Whip cream to soft peaks, fold into cheese mixture.
  4. Spread over base, chill minimum 4 hours.
  5. Hull and halve strawberries. Warm jam with lemon juice, brush over berries.
  6. Arrange on cheesecake just before serving.
⏱ 4 hrs ⭐ Medium

Salted Caramel Brownies

Ingredients

  • 200g dark chocolate · 175g butter
  • 300g caster sugar · 3 large eggs
  • 125g plain flour · 50g cocoa powder
  • Caramel: 150g caster sugar, 80ml double cream, 50g butter, 1 tsp sea salt flakes

Method

  1. Make caramel: melt sugar until amber, add cream and butter. Stir in salt. Cool.
  2. Preheat oven 180°C. Line a 20cm square tin.
  3. Melt chocolate and butter. Beat in sugar, then eggs.
  4. Fold in flour and cocoa. Pour into tin.
  5. Drizzle caramel over, swirl with a skewer.
  6. Bake 20–25 min. Cool before cutting.
⏱ 45 min ⭐ Easy

French Macarons

Ingredients

  • 150g ground almonds · 150g icing sugar
  • 2 × 55g aged egg whites
  • 150g caster sugar · 50ml water
  • Gel food colouring (optional)
  • Filling: 100g butter, 200g icing sugar, 2 tbsp raspberry jam

Method

  1. Blitz almonds and icing sugar to a fine powder. Sift.
  2. Make Italian meringue: boil sugar and water to 118°C. Pour onto whipping egg whites.
  3. Fold meringue into almond mixture (macaronage).
  4. Pipe 3.5cm rounds on lined baking sheets. Leave 45 min to skin.
  5. Bake 140°C for 14 min. Cool fully.
  6. Sandwich with buttercream. Mature in fridge 24 hours.
⏱ 2 hrs ⭐ Hard

Cinnamon Rolls

Ingredients

  • Dough: 500g strong white flour, 7g yeast, 60g sugar, 1 tsp salt, 60g butter, 250ml warm milk, 2 eggs
  • Filling: 75g butter, 100g brown sugar, 3 tsp cinnamon
  • Icing: 200g cream cheese, 150g icing sugar, 1 tsp vanilla, 2 tbsp milk

Method

  1. Make dough, knead 10 min. Rise 1 hour until doubled.
  2. Roll out to 40×30cm rectangle. Spread with soft butter.
  3. Mix sugar and cinnamon, sprinkle over. Roll up tightly, cut into 12 rolls.
  4. Place in greased tin. Rise 45 min.
  5. Bake 180°C for 22–25 min until golden.
  6. Drizzle with cream cheese icing while warm.
⏱ 2.5 hrs ⭐ Medium

⏱ 3 hrs⭐ Medium

Raspberry Tart

Ingredients

  • Pastry: 200g plain flour, 100g cold butter, 50g icing sugar, 1 egg yolk
  • Crème pâtissière: 500ml milk, 4 egg yolks, 100g sugar, 40g cornflour, 30g butter, 1 tsp vanilla
  • 350g fresh raspberries · 3 tbsp seedless raspberry jam (to glaze)

Method

  1. Make pastry, chill 30 min. Blind bake 180°C for 15 min, remove beans, bake 10 min more.
  2. Make crème pâtissière: warm milk; whisk yolks, sugar, cornflour; pour in milk; stir over heat until thick; add butter and vanilla. Cool completely.
  3. Fill cooled tart shell with crème pâtissière.
  4. Arrange raspberries on top in concentric circles.
  5. Warm jam, brush over raspberries to glaze.
⏱ 3 hrs ⭐ Medium

Chocolate Chip Cookies

⏱ 30 min⭐ Easy
Tap to flip ↩

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 225g unsalted butter, softened
  • 175g caster sugar · 175g light brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs · 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 350g plain flour · 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda · 1 tsp salt
  • 300g chocolate chips (dark and milk mixed)

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C. Line baking trays.
  2. Cream butter and sugars until light (3–4 min).
  3. Beat in eggs and vanilla.
  4. Mix in flour, bicarb, and salt. Fold in chocolate chips.
  5. Scoop heaped tablespoons onto trays, spaced well apart.
  6. Bake 10–12 min until golden edges but still soft centres. Cool on tray 5 min.
⏱ 30 min ⭐ Easy
